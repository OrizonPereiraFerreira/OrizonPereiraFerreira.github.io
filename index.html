About

I am a Professor at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics of the Federal University of Goiás. My research is on theory and methods for continuous optimization, with special interest in optimization methods designed for Riemannian manifolds.

I received a Ph.D. in Systems Engineering and Computing from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro in 1997, a master's degree in Mathematics from IMPA and a degree in Mathematics from the Federal University of Goiás. Code for my recent papers is on GitHub, one repository per paper.
